Instructions

Version 1.0

Overview

There are 44 notes on this hammered dulcimer.

Your goal is to get them all in tune as quickly as you can.

Getting Started and Difficulty

Each time you open or reload the game, choose your difficulty and click Start Tuning to begin a fresh game and start the timer.

Choose Start Over to begin a new game and select a difficulty level.

Spot Tuning detunes 3 randomly selected strings.

Very Easy detunes 6 randomly selected strings (approximately one eighth).

Easy detunes 11 strings, Medium detunes 22, and Hard detunes all 44.

Outdoor Festival starts with six detuned strings and simulates unexpected events that might happen at an outdoor folk music festival, causing additional strings to go out of tune as you play.

Random detunes a randomly selected number from 1 to 44.

Tuning the Dulcimer

Tap or click the note you want to tune.

The selected note is marked red when out of tune or green when in tune.

Use the Tune Up or Tune Down arrows to bring the selected note into tune.

Compare notes by ear using the dulcimer's repeated pitches and musical intervals.

Left to Tune shows how many notes are still out of tune.

Watch for Too High and Too Low warnings, and be careful not to break a string by tuning it too high.

Use the sound icon to stop all sounds.

Best Times

Your best time for the current level appears below the timer once you complete a game at that level.

Each difficulty level has its own best time, which is preserved when you switch levels.

Use Reset Best Time for This Level in the startup or Start Over dialog to reset the selected level's best time after confirmation.

Your selected difficulty and best times are saved in your browser.

Phones and Tablets

On iPhone and iPad, tap Enable Playback after the samples have loaded.

For the largest playing surface, use a tablet or turn your phone to landscape orientation.
