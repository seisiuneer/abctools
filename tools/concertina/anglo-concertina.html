Version 1.5

Overview

Anglo Concertina is a web-based port of ConcertinaXL for iOS and simulates a 30-button C/G Anglo concertina using the original sampled concertina sounds.

The tool is designed primarily for playing with a computer keyboard.

Computer Keyboard to Buttons Map

Press a key to play a note and release it to stop.

Hold Space to push the bellows and release Space to draw.

Held notes change pitch automatically when the bellows direction changes.

Play multiple keys for chords.

Escape stops all notes.

Keyboard Rollover and Polyphony

Anglo Concertina can play multiple notes at the same time, but some computer keyboards cannot detect every combination of keys pressed together.

This limitation, known as keyboard rollover, may cause some notes in a chord not to sound, even though other chords with the same number of notes play correctly.

Holding the space bar for push also counts as an additional key press.

For the best results when playing chords with both hands, use a keyboard that supports full N-key rollover (NKRO), a feature available on many gaming keyboards.

Mouse

Click and hold any concertina button to play it using the current bellows direction.

With the mouse button held down, slide between note buttons to play different notes.

Click and hold either bottom corner below the note buttons to push the bellows while playing notes with the computer keyboard.

Release the mouse button to draw unless Space is still held.

Settings

Click the information (i) button to change the instrument style, transposition, volume, reverb, and note labels.

Select Jeffries, Modified Jeffries, or Wheatstone to match your concertina layout.

Jeffries has D#4 on the Push and C#4 on the Draw of the first right-hand accidental-row button (Y), and B5 on the Push and F#5 on the Draw of the outermost right-hand G-row button (/).

Modified Jeffries is the default and differs from Jeffries in two Push notes: C#4 instead of D#4 on Y, and F5 instead of B5 on /.

Both styles have identical Draw notes.

Wheatstone uses the Wheatstone/Lachenal accidental-row layout.

Transposition changes the playback pitch only; the note labels always show the original C/G concertina notes for the current bellows direction.

Settings are saved automatically in your browser.

iOS and Android

Anglo Concertina is designed primarily for use with a physical computer keyboard, including an external keyboard connected to an iOS or Android device.

To play without a keyboard, touch and hold either bottom corner of the concertina below the note buttons to push the bellows, and release to draw.

Use another finger to play notes while holding a corner, or play several notes at once using multiple fingers.

Slide a finger between note buttons without lifting it to play different notes.

Both corner areas work the same way, so you can use either thumb.

On iPhone and iPad, tap Enable Playback after the note samples have loaded.
