Overview

English Concertina is a web-based port of Englitina XL, simulating a 48-button English concertina using the original sampled instrument sounds.

Play using a computer keyboard, mouse, or touchscreen.

Computer Keyboard

Each hand uses four computer keys per row to match the four buttons in each instrument row.

Low covers the lowest four rows, Middle covers the middle four rows, and High covers the highest four rows.

The instrument starts in the Middle range each time it is opened.

Select a range instantly using either hand:

Press Space to cycle from Low to Middle to High and back to Low.

The selected range stays active until you select another range.

By default, buttons outside the selected computer keyboard range are dimmed, but remain playable by touch or mouse.

Notes already sounding continue unchanged until their playing keys are released.

All 48 buttons remain available to the mouse and touchscreen in every keyboard range.

Mouse and Touchscreen

Click and hold a button with the mouse or touch it with a finger to play a note.

Slide between buttons without lifting your finger or releasing the mouse button to change notes.

Moving off the buttons stops the note until you slide onto another button.

On a touchscreen, use multiple fingers to play chords or slide between notes independently.

Keyboard Rollover and Polyphony

English Concertina can play multiple notes at once, but some computer keyboards cannot detect every combination of keys pressed together.

For best results playing chords, use a keyboard with full N-key rollover (NKRO).

Settings

Click the information (i) button to adjust transposition, volume, reverb, and note labels.

Transposition changes only the playback pitch; the original note labels and keyboard mappings remain unchanged.

Set Show Ranges to Disabled to remove the range dimming, which may be useful when playing on a touchscreen.

This setting affects only the visual appearance, not the keyboard range or the highlighting of sounding notes.

All settings are saved automatically in your browser.

iOS and Android

All 48 buttons can be played directly on the touchscreen, without a physical keyboard.

You can also use a physical keyboard connected to your device.

On iPhone and iPad, tap Enable Playback after the samples have loaded.
