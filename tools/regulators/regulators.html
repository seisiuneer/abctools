Instructions

Playing the Drones

Tap the drone puck to turn the drones on or off.

On iPhone and iPad, tap Enable Playback when first opening the tool.

Playing the Regulators

Play the virtual uilleann pipe regulators by touching or clicking the brass keys.

In single-regulator mode, you can play one note in each of the three columns at the same time using multiple fingers.

Slide along the keys to change notes, and lift your fingers to stop them.

Paired Regulator Mode

Tap the 1/2 regulator button to switch between single-regulator and paired-chord modes.

In paired mode, the left half of the keys plays tenor and baritone chords, and the right half plays bass and baritone chords.

Slide along the keys to change chords, and lift your finger to stop them.

Settings

Tap the information button on the lower left to select pitch, drone style, top chord, fine tuning, individual volumes, and reverb.

All settings, including the playing mode, are saved automatically in your browser.

Computer Keyboard

Left hand: Q W E R T for tenor, A S D F for baritone, Z X C V for bass.

Right hand: Y U I O P for tenor, H J K L for baritone, N M , . for bass.

In paired mode, the tenor keys (Q–T or Y–P) play tenor/baritone pairs, and the baritone keys (A–F or H–L) play baritone/bass pairs.

The bass keyboard keys are inactive in paired mode.

Space toggles drones when the page body is focused.
