Instructions

Version 1.2

Introduction

Regulators simulates the drones and the tenor, baritone, and bass regulators of a full set of Irish Uilleann pipes in five keys: D, C#, C, B, and Bb.

All drone and regulator sounds are sampled from actual instruments.

Playing the Drones

Tap the drone puck to turn the drones on or off.

Playing the Regulators

Play the virtual uilleann pipe regulators by touching or clicking the brass keys.

In single-regulator mode, you can play one note in each of the three regulator rows or columns at the same time using multiple fingers.

Slide along the keys to change notes, and lift your fingers to stop them.

Paired Regulator Mode

Tap the regulator mode button next to the drone button to switch between single-regulator and paired-regulator modes.

In paired mode, the tenor side plays tenor and baritone chords, and the bass side plays bass and baritone chords.

Slide along the keys to change chords, and lift your finger to stop them.

Settings

Tap the information (i) button to select pitch, drone style, top chord, fine tuning, individual volumes, reverb, instrument orientation, and note labels.

Set Labels to On to show the note names on the regulator keys, referenced to a D set of pipes regardless of the selected pitch.

All settings, including the playing mode, are saved automatically in your browser.

Instrument Orientation

Use the Orientation selector in Settings to choose Left, Vertical, or Right.

Vertical (default) displays the regulators in their original upright arrangement.

Right rotates the instrument counterclockwise for horizontal playing.

Left displays the mirrored horizontal arrangement for a left-handed set of pipes.

Computer Keyboard

The keyboard layout is the same in all instrument orientations.

The diagram shows the note played by each computer key in single-regulator mode, using the note names of a D set of pipes.

In paired mode, the tenor keys play tenor/baritone pairs, and the baritone keys play baritone/bass pairs.

The bass keyboard keys are inactive in paired mode.

In paired mode, the Top chord setting selects C/G or A/C for the highest pair on either side, with the note names referenced to a D set.

Space toggles drones when the page body is focused.

iOS Notes

On iPhone and iPad, tap Enable Playback when first opening the tool.
