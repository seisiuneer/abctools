Tremolo Reed Sample Converter Instructions

Overview

Load a complete chromatic set of single-note WAV or MP3 reed-instrument samples and create two-reed tremolo versions of them. The converter analyzes the samples, sorts them from low pitch to high pitch, applies the selected tremolo settings, normalizes the finished samples, and exports them together in a ZIP file.

The selected files must contain every semitone from the lowest note through the highest note, with one sample per semitone. If a note is missing or duplicated, the files are not opened for processing and the converter reports the problem.

Beat Offset

Starting Beat Offset sets the total frequency separation between the two reeds at the lowest note. Ending Beat Offset sets the total separation at the highest note.

Beat Offset Curve controls how that separation changes across the chromatic sample range. Linear uses an even progression. Curves 1–5 provide progressively stronger nonlinear curves. The graph shows the selected progression from the starting value to the ending value.

Reed Tuning and Volume

Reed Tuning selects how the two reed frequencies are created. − / + Offset splits the total beat offset equally below and above the original pitch. Original / + Offset leaves the first reed at the original frequency and shifts the second reed upward by the full offset.

Reed Volume Balance controls the relative volume of the two generated reeds. At 50 / 50, both reeds contribute equally.

Output Level

LUFS Target sets the loudness target for each finished tremolo sample. The default is −15 LUFS. Normalization is applied after the two reeds are mixed.

Output Level Trim applies an additional gain adjustment after normalization.

Output samples are mono, 44.1 kHz IEEE 32-bit floating-point WAV files. A short end fade is applied to help prevent boundary clicks.

Sample List and Pitch Detection

The sample list is shown in detected pitch order. It displays the source LUFS value, detected note and fundamental frequency, total Hz and cents offsets, and the individual reed frequencies and cents offsets used for that sample.

Pitch detection analyzes a relatively steady central portion of each sample. Recognizable filename note names such as C4, F#4, Bb3, and A_440 are also used when available. If a detected pitch is wrong, enter the correct fundamental frequency in Hz before previewing or converting.

Preview

Select a sample from the Preview menu, or use Previous and Next to move through the samples. Play Original plays the source sample and Play Tremolo previews the generated tremolo using the current Beat Offset Curve, Reed Tuning, Reed Volume Balance, and output settings.

Original and Tremolo previews automatically loop continuously until stopped. While a preview is playing, its Play button changes to Stop Play; click it again to stop. Starting Original while Tremolo is playing, or Tremolo while Original is playing, automatically stops the current preview first. Selecting a different note also stops playback.

If a tremolo preview is playing and you change a tremolo or output setting, the converter briefly debounces the change, regenerates the preview with the new setting, and restarts that tremolo note.

Converting and Saving

Click Convert All & Download ZIP to process the complete sample set. Enter the ZIP filename when prompted. Each generated WAV keeps the same filename as its original sample, with a .wav extension.
