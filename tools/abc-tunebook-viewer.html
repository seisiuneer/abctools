ABC Tunebook Viewer — Instructions

Introduction

ABC Tunebook Viewer provides a simple way to browse, play, and edit tunes from an ABC tunebook.

Open ABC files directly, or use Open in Tunebook Viewer from the ABC Transcription Tools menu to send all the tunes currently loaded there.

Once tunes are opened, the list of tunes is shown on the left and the selected tune's notation is on the right.

Open

Choose one or more .abc or .txt files to load their tunes.

Opening files replaces the current tunebook.

If you have added, deleted, sorted, transposed, or edited any tunes, you will be asked before replacing them so your changes are not lost accidentally.

You can also drag and drop .abc or .txt files anywhere on the page.

Use Add Tune above the search bar to add a tune at the end of the list.

Its ABC opens for editing with standard header fields ready to fill in.

Use Delete Tune to remove the selected tune after confirmation.

Use Clear to remove all tunes; unsaved changes require confirmation.

Show tune info

When checked, the meter (M:), rhythm (R:), and normalized key (K:) appear below each tune title when available, for example 6/8 - Jig - E Dorian.

Uncheck to show only titles.

Your preference is saved in the browser.

Find

Filter the list by title, then select a title to show that tune.

Sort

Click Sort, choose a tag, then click Sort to reorder the tunes.

Choose Restore to Original Order to return tunes to their loaded order while preserving added or deleted tunes, ABC edits, and transpositions.

Sorting uses the current ABC, including updated titles and keys.

The first tune is selected after sorting or restoring.

Resize the tune list

Drag the divider to make the list wider (or taller on phones).

Your setting is saved automatically.

Double-click or double-tap the divider to reset.

Display mode

Choose notation, names, or instrument tablature.

Your choice is saved for your next visit.

ABC

Click ABC in the notation header, just to the left of the Transpose buttons, to edit the selected tune's ABC directly.

The notation updates shortly after you stop typing, making the Viewer a useful light ABC editor, though not as full-featured as ABC Transcription Tools.

Edits are kept when switching tunes and are included when saving or sharing.

The panel edits one tune at a time. To replace it, select all the ABC text and paste a complete single tune, including its X: line. The ABC must contain exactly one X: line; deleting it or pasting multiple tunes is not allowed.

Use Font size to adjust the text. Drag the title bar to move the window, or drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.

Transpose the selected tune

Use Transpose ↓ or Transpose ↑ in the notation header.

Click to transpose the selected tune one semitone, Shift-click for two semitones, or Alt-click for one octave (12 semitones).

Alt takes priority if both modifier keys are held.

The notation, playback, key signature, and chord symbols are updated together.

Transpose changes are cumulative and kept for each tune when switching tunes, sorting, or restoring the original tune order.

Saving, sharing, creating lessons, and sending tunes to other tools all use the transposed ABC.

Instruments

Choose the playback soundfont and the melody, bass, and chords MIDI instruments.

Set the bass and chords volumes from 0 to 127.

With Override all MIDI programs and volumes checked (the default), the selected values replace MIDI programs and volumes specified in each tune during playback.

Uncheck it to keep any MIDI programs or volumes already present in a tune and use your selected defaults only for missing settings.

These changes apply only during playback; the original ABC is never modified.

Your instrument choices and override setting are saved and restored on your next visit.

Tempo

The Tempo button opens the same tempo percentage dialog as the playback bar.

Playback

Use the controls below the notation to play, pause, restart, loop, or seek within a tune.

Click the tempo percentage field to set playback speed from 10% to 200%.

The tempo setting is saved in this browser.

Full page

Expand the notation and playback across the page.

Click Close full page or press Escape to return.

Saving and Sharing Tunes

Click Share to save tunes as an ABC file, create a share link, or create a lesson.

Save Current Tune to ABC File saves only the tune selected in the sidebar, including its ABC file header when present and any transposition you made.

Save All Tunes to ABC File saves the entire loaded collection, including any transposed tunes, in the order shown in the sidebar.

This is useful for saving a sorted tunebook or combining tunes opened from several files into one ABC file.

Both options preserve applicable ABC file headers and separate tunes with blank lines.

On iPhone or iPad, use the share sheet to save the new file.

Share Current Tune and Share All Tunes create links you can copy, save, or test.

Sharing options are unavailable when the selected ABC is too large for a single or all ABC tunes link; saving ABC files remains available.

Lessons let you share a single tune or the complete tunebook along with a lesson title and instructions.

Choose Configure Lesson (Current tune) or Configure Lesson (All tunes), enter your instructions, then create a lesson link for your students.

When students open the link, they see your lesson title and instructions before selecting Start Lesson to browse and play the tunes.

You can also save a lesson configuration to a file and reopen it later.

In the Share dialog, choose Open in ABCTT (Current tune) or Open in ABCTT (All tunes) to send tunes to ABC Transcription Tools for editing, provided the ABC fits in the share link.

Open in Tool (Current tune) or Open in Tool (All tunes) sends your ABC to another music tool.

For complete instructions and demonstration videos, see:

ABC Transcription Tools User Guide — Open Tunes in Another Tool.

Lessons for Teachers

Choose Share → Configure Lesson (Current tune) or Configure Lesson (All tunes), enter a title and instructions, then click Create Lesson Link.

Save Lesson Configuration saves the tune collection and lesson settings to a file; use Load Lesson Configuration to restore it later.

You can also open a saved lesson file using Open.

Lessons for Students

Opening a lesson link displays the teacher's title and instructions.

Click Start Lesson to enable playback and browse the tunes.

You can change the notation display mode during the lesson without changing your saved preference.

Add Tune, Delete Tune, Clear, and editing ABC are disabled during lessons.
