ABC Tunebook Viewer — Instructions

Introduction

ABC Tunebook Viewer provides a simple way to browse and play tunes from an ABC tunebook.

Open ABC files directly, or use Open in Tunebook Viewer from the ABC Transcription Tools menu to send all the tunes currently loaded there.

Overview

Browse a collection of ABC tunes with the tune list on the left and the selected tune's notation and playback on the right.

Open

Choose one or more .abc or .txt files to load their tunes.

Opening files replaces the current tunebook.

You can also drag and drop .abc or .txt files anywhere on the page.

Show tune info

When checked, the meter (M:), rhythm (R:), and normalized key (K:) appear below each tune title when available, for example 6/8 - Jig - E Dorian.

Uncheck to show only titles.

Your preference is saved in the browser.

Resize the tune list

Drag the divider to make the list wider (or taller on phones).

Your setting is saved automatically.

Double-click or double-tap the divider to reset.

Find

Filter the list by title, then select a title to show that tune.

Display mode

Choose notation, names, or instrument tablature.

Your choice is saved for your next visit.

Playback

Use the controls below the notation to play, pause, restart, loop, or seek within a tune.

Click the tempo percentage field to set playback speed from 10% to 200%.

The tempo setting is saved in this browser.

Instruments

Choose the playback soundfont and the melody, bass, and chords MIDI instruments.

Set the bass and chords volumes from 0 to 127.

With Override all MIDI programs and volumes checked (the default), the selected values replace MIDI programs and volumes specified in each tune during playback.

Uncheck it to keep any MIDI programs or volumes already present in a tune and use your selected defaults only for missing settings.

These changes apply only during playback; the original ABC is never modified.

Your instrument choices and override setting are saved and restored on your next visit.

Tempo

The Tempo button opens the same tempo percentage dialog as the playback bar.

Open in ABCTT

Open the selected tune in the ABC Transcription Tools editor in a new browser tab.

The share link uses the original ABC (without any playback-only MIDI changes) and carries the current display mode.

ABC

Click ABC to view the selected tune's ABC text. It is read-only, but you can select and copy it.

Use Font size to adjust the text. Drag the title bar to move the window, or drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.

Full page

Expand the notation and playback across the page.

Click Close full page or press Escape to return.
