ABC Tunebook Viewer — Instructions

Browse a collection of ABC tunes with the list on the left and the selected tune's notation and playback on the right.

  • Open: Choose one or more .abc or .txt files. Their tunes replace the current tunebook. Drag and drop files anywhere on the page to do the same.
  • Show tune info: When checked, show the meter (M:), rhythm (R:), and normalized key (K:) below each tune title when available, for example 6/8 - Jig - E Dorian. Uncheck to show only titles. This preference is saved in your browser.
  • Find: Filter the list by title, then click a title to show that tune.
  • Display mode: Choose notation, names, or instrument tablature. A manual choice is saved for your next visit. A mode sent from ABC Transcription Tools is temporary until you change it yourself.
  • Playback: Use the controls below the notation to play/pause, restart, loop and seek. Click the tempo percentage field to set a value from 10% to 200%. Tempo is saved in this browser.
  • Tempo: Opens the same tempo percentage dialog as the field in the playback bar.
  • Full page: Expand the notation and playback across the page, then click Close full page or press Escape to return.
