ABC Fretboard Player Instructions

Overview

Load, type, or paste ABC tunes and play them with synchronized standard notation and optional tablature for the selected fretted instrument.

The fretboard pane shows the current tune title and, during playback, the selected instrument’s fingering, with the note name and optional staff notation to its right.

Use Instrument to select Mandolin, Tenor Banjo, Bouzouki (GDAE), Bouzouki (GDAD), Guitar (Standard), or Guitar (DADGAD).

The instrument selection is saved and restored.

Use Capo to select a capo position from 0 through 12.

The capo is applied to generated tablature and the live fretboard display, is saved and restored, and is included in share links and transfers back to ABC Transcription Tools.

The string labels in both fretboard views show the strings' actual sounding pitch with the selected capo, using sharps or flats according to the tune's K: key and mode.

The notation tablature and both live fingering views show fret numbers relative to the capo, so the capo is treated as fret 0.

The string pitch labels still show the strings' actual sounding pitches at the selected capo position.

Use Notation display to choose standard notation alone or standard notation with tablature for the selected instrument.

The default is Notation + Tab.

Opening and Selecting Tunes

Type or paste a single tune into the ABC editor, open an .abc or .txt file containing one or more tunes, or send tunes directly from ABC Transcription Tools.

For multi-tune files, use the Tune menu to select the tune to display and play.

Edits are retained when changing tunes.

The ABC editor intentionally accepts only one pasted tune at a time; multi-tune collections should be opened as a file or transferred from ABC Transcription Tools.

ABC Editing and Notation

The upper-left panel contains editable ABC.

The lower-left panel shows abcjs notation and playback controls.

The Notation display selector chooses whether tablature for the selected instrument is also shown.

Editing the ABC rebuilds the notation and audio.

When tablature is enabled it is rebuilt at the same time.

Click a rendered note to select its corresponding ABC text and show that note in the fingering display.

The Small / Medium / Large / Largest selector changes the ABC editor text size and remembers the choice in this browser.

Fretboard Display

With Show Fretboard checked, the current note is shown on a vertical fretboard.

Black circles show fretted notes and the hollow circle shows the effective open-string position at the nut or capo.

The nut or capo is treated as fret 0, and all displayed fret numbers are relative to that position, matching the abcjs tablature.

Notes above the visible fretboard are also labeled with their capo-relative fret number.

The string labels above the fretboard show the actual sounding pitches at the selected capo position.

Uncheck Show Fretboard to use the compact string-and-fret display; it uses the same capo-relative fret numbers and capo-adjusted sounding string pitches.

This setting is remembered in this browser and is not included in share links.

Check Show note to also show the current pitch as a quarter note on a staff below the note name.

The Show note setting is remembered in this browser and is not included in share links.

Note names use sharps or flats according to the tune's K: key and mode, with a fixed octave format: G3-B3 use a trailing comma, C4-B4 use capital letters, C5-B5 use a trailing apostrophe, and C6-B6 use two trailing apostrophes.

When Show note is enabled, the staff display uses the same sharp or flat naming.

Check Hide to clear the instrument display; it can be used if you want to practice a tune without seeing the instrument fingerings. Uncheck Hide to reveal the display again. This setting is remembered during normal use and can be changed without saving during a lesson.

The Notation display, Instrument, and Capo choices are remembered in this browser and are also included in generated share links.

Playback and Tempo

As each melody note plays, the notation cursor follows the tune and the fretboard pane updates to that note.

Playback uses the ABC Transcription Tools custom MIDI program 141 for Mandolin, MIDI program 105 for Tenor Banjo, the ABC Transcription Tools custom MIDI program 140 for either Bouzouki tuning, or MIDI program 25 for either Guitar tuning, with program 0 for bass and chords, overriding matching playback directives in the source ABC.

Check Metronome to add a meter-appropriate metronome click during playback.

Changing this setting stops and rewinds the current tune.

The metronome setting is remembered in this browser but is not included in share links.

Check Count in? to show a five-second countdown before playback starts when you click Play while playback is stopped.

The Count in setting is remembered in this browser.

Click Mute Melody in the Notation header to mute the melody.

This is available for every tune, including tunes without chord symbols, so you can practice with the metronome alone.

If the tune contains chord symbols, chord and bass accompaniment remain audible.

Toggling Mute Melody or Unmute Melody stops and rewinds the tune.

If it was playing, playback restarts from the beginning after the five-second count-in when Count in? is enabled, or immediately when Count in is disabled.

If the tune was stopped, it remains stopped.

The button changes to Unmute Melody; click it again to restore the melody.

The melody always starts unmuted when the tool is opened, and the mute setting resets to unmuted any time you change tunes or load a new tune collection.

Click Tempo in the Notation header to set playback tempo from 10% to 200% of the tune's original tempo. The value is remembered in this browser.

Resizing and Full-Page View

On wide screens, drag the vertical divider to resize the left and fretboard panes, and drag the horizontal divider to resize the ABC and notation panes.

Double-click a divider to reset it.

Click Full-Page View on the ABC, Notation, or Fretboard pane to expand it to the full browser window.

Click Exit Full-Page to return to the normal pane view.

Saving and Sharing

Click Save to save the complete currently loaded tune collection, including edits.

On mobile devices the saved file uses a .txt extension.

Share opens the Sharing Controls dialog. Choose Share Current Tune for the current tune or Share All Tunes for the complete loaded collection.

The Sharing Controls dialog closes and the selected link opens in a Share Link dialog where you can copy it, save it as a text file, test it in a new browser tab, or open the ABC in the ABC Transcription Tools. Click Close to close the Share Link dialog.

Open in ABCTT opens the selected ABC in the ABC Transcription Tools ABC Editor in a new browser tab, using the player's normal instrument-specific conversion settings. For a Lesson Share Link, lesson-only information is omitted and only the tune and applicable instrument settings are sent to ABC Transcription Tools.

Fretboard Player share links include the current Notation display, Instrument, and Capo settings so they are restored when the link is opened.

Settings received from a share link are used for that visit without changing your saved preferences. If you change any player setting, the current share-link settings become your saved preferences too. Opening a file with Browse loads its ABC only and does not change player settings.

The current capo value is passed to ABC Transcription Tools.

When ABC arrives through a share link or direct transfer, click Click to Enable Playback if the browser requires a user gesture before audio can start.

Lesson Information for Music Teachers

Share opens the Sharing Controls dialog. Choose Configure Lesson… to create, edit, save, load, or share a lesson for your students.

A lesson uses the current tune only.

Enter a lesson title and instructions in the Configure Lesson dialog.

The lesson title and instructions are remembered in this browser while you work and are restored the next time you open Configure Lesson.

Save Lesson Configuration saves a reusable text file containing the current tune, lesson title, instructions, and the same player settings carried by a normal Fretboard Player share link.

Load Lesson Configuration can open either a saved Lesson Configuration file or a Lesson Link saved as a text file.

Those saved player settings are Notation display, Instrument, and Capo.

You can also open a saved Lesson Configuration with the main ABC file Browse button. In this case, only the ABC tune is loaded; the lesson title, instructions, and saved player settings are not applied.

If one or more tunes are already loaded, the tool asks for confirmation before replacing them with the lesson tune.

Loading a lesson configuration restores its tune, lesson title, instructions, and saved player settings. The restored player settings also become your saved preferences for future use.

Create Lesson Link creates a share link for the current tune containing the lesson title and instructions plus the same Notation display, Instrument, and Capo settings carried by a normal share link.

The generated lesson link opens in the same Share Link dialog, stacked over the Configure Lesson dialog, where it can be copied to the clipboard, saved as a text file, tested in a new browser tab, or opened in the ABC Transcription Tools with lesson-only parameters removed. Click Back to close the Lesson Share Link dialog and return to Configure Lesson.

The Hide checkbox beside Show note lets you prepare a lesson with the instrument display initially hidden, so students can practice without seeing the instrument fingerings. Its checked or unchecked state is included in Lesson Links and saved Lesson Configurations. Students can change Hide during the lesson.

Lesson Information for Students

Your music teacher may provide you with a Fretboard Player lesson link.

Open the lesson link in your web browser to load the tune, lesson instructions, and the player settings chosen by your teacher.

Read the lesson information that appears, then click Start Lesson when you are ready to begin.

After clicking Start Lesson, click Click to Enable Playback to enable the audio playback controls.

The lesson's Notation display, Instrument, and Capo settings and the lesson tune selector are shown at the top of the player but are initially disabled so the teacher's lesson setup cannot be changed.

The Browse and Save controls remain available while you are using a lesson, but the Share control is hidden so you cannot create share links or lessons.

You can use the normal notation, fretboard, playback, tempo, metronome, count-in, display, and pane controls that remain available while practicing the lesson.

You can edit the ABC text during the lesson if needed.

Click Save to save your edited ABC.

You can use Browse to load a saved ABC file back into the player or open another ABC file. The lesson's player settings remain locked. After you load an ABC file, the tune selector is unlocked so you can select any tune in that file.

To return to normal Fretboard Player operation, open the player normally rather than through the lesson link.

The Hide checkbox beside Show note may be initially checked by your teacher to let you practice without seeing the instrument fingerings. Uncheck Hide whenever you want to reveal the instrument display, or check it again to practice without it. Changing Hide during a lesson does not change your saved preference for normal player use.
