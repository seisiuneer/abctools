ABC Mountain Dulcimer Player Instructions

Overview

Load, type, or paste ABC tunes to view standard notation, Mountain Dulcimer tablature, and a live three-course fingering display.

The player follows the tune during playback and shows where each melody note can be played on your dulcimer.

Use Notation display to choose Notation Only or Notation + Tab.

Setting Up Your Mountain Dulcimer

Choose the tuning that matches your instrument using Tuning.

The table shows the open-string pitches from bass to melody, before applying a capo.

Set Capo from 0 (no capo) to 7 to match the physical fret where your capo is placed.

A dulcimer has diatonic frets, so moving the capo by one fret does not necessarily raise the pitch by one semitone.

The string labels in the notation tablature and fretboard show the notes sounding with the selected capo.

The displayed fret numbers identify physical fret positions on the dulcimer.

Choose Highest fret to match your instrument, and check 1½ or 6½ under Extra frets only if your dulcimer has those frets.

Your instrument settings are remembered in this browser.

Opening and Selecting Tunes

Use Browse to open an .abc or .txt file containing one or more tunes.

You can also type or paste a single ABC tune into the editor or receive tunes from ABC Transcription Tools.

For a file with several tunes, use the Tune menu to choose which tune to display and play.

Edits to tunes are retained when you switch between tunes.

ABC Editing and Notation

Edit the ABC text in the upper-left pane to change notes, titles, keys, or other tune details.

The notation and, when enabled, tablature update to reflect your edits.

This single-melody player rejects tunes containing voice declarations (V: or [V:]), multiple staves, bracketed note chords such as [CEG], or inline key changes such as [K:G]. An unsupported tune displays a warning instead of notation, tablature, or playback controls.

Use the editor text-size menu to make the ABC text easier to read.

Click a note in the notation to show its fingering on the dulcimer display.

Use the previous and next note controls to step through the tune without playing it.

If a note cannot be played with the selected tuning, capo, and frets, the tablature shows a red X.

Use the missing-note navigation controls to find notes that are not playable as written.

The player may choose among several possible strings and frets for a note, generally favoring the melody string.

Fretboard Display and Navigation

The Mountain Dulcimer panel shows the current note and its suggested string and fret position.

Use Show note to display the note name, and Hide to practice without seeing the fingering.

Click Full-Page View to enlarge a pane, and Exit Full-Page to return to the normal layout.

Playback and Tempo

Use the playback controls below the notation to play, pause, change tempo, or repeat the tune.

During playback, the highlighted notation and dulcimer display follow the melody.

Enable Metronome for a steady beat, or Count in? for a countdown before playback.

Click Configure Playback in the Notation header to choose the instrument sound and adjust the playback options.

Choose Dulcimer (default), Guitar, or Harp as the instrument sound.

The selected instrument sound applies to both the melody and the open strings.

Play octave lower? plays the melody one octave below its written pitch without changing the displayed notation.

This option also uses the lower melody pitch when determining playable fretboard positions and generating tablature.

Play open strings? sounds the dulcimer's open strings along with each melody note, giving the tune the accompanying drone sound characteristic of the instrument.

This option is enabled by default, and the open-string pitches follow the selected tuning and capo position.

When both options are enabled, the melody plays an octave lower while the open strings retain their actual tuned pitches.

Use Mute Melody to practice without hearing the melody; any enabled metronome and open strings remain audible.

If the browser asks you to enable audio, click Click to Enable Playback before playing.

Saving and Sharing

Click Save to download the loaded ABC tunes, including your edits.

Click Share to open Sharing Controls and choose Share Current Tune or Share All Tunes.

In the Share Link dialog, you can copy the link, save it, or test it in a new browser tab.

Share links carry the selected tuning, capo, highest fret, extra frets, notation display, initial Play open strings? and Play octave lower? settings, and Instrument selection so the recipient can use the same setup.

Use Open in ABCTT to open the ABC in ABC Transcription Tools for further editing.

A received share link applies its settings for that visit without replacing your saved preferences unless you change a setting.

Tablature Solution Limitations

The tool is limited to generating tablature solutions automatically for melody-only tunes for a variety of Mountain Dulcimer tunings.

It is not able to automatically generate tablature for arrangements that include ABC bracket-style chords, such as [CEG].

Printing

Use your browser's Print command to print the music notation and, when enabled, Mountain Dulcimer tablature.

The print layout is optimized for US Letter paper in portrait orientation and automatically reduces the notation size to help tunes fit on the page.

Very long tunes may require multiple pages, and printing results may vary depending on the browser.

Lesson Information for Music Teachers

Click Share, then choose Configure Lesson… to create a lesson for the current tune.

Enter a lesson title and instructions for your students.

Use Save Lesson Configuration to save the tune, lesson details, and selected player settings for later reuse.

Use Load Lesson Configuration to restore a saved lesson configuration or lesson link file.

Click Create Lesson Link to generate a link that you can send to students.

Lesson links include the selected tuning, capo, highest fret, extra frets, initial notation display, initial Play open strings? and Play octave lower? settings, and Instrument selection.

The tuning, capo, fret, and Play octave lower? settings are locked during a lesson so students practice with the setup you selected.

Use Hide if you want the fingering display to start hidden; students can reveal it while practicing.

Lesson Information for Students

Open the lesson link provided by your teacher to load the assigned tune, lesson instructions, and dulcimer settings.

Read the instructions and click Start Lesson to begin.

If prompted, click Click to Enable Playback to turn on the audio controls.

You can change Notation display, and use Configure Playback to change the Instrument and Play open strings? options without changing your saved preferences.

Your teacher's tuning, capo, fret, and Play octave lower? settings remain locked during the lesson.

You can use the notation, note navigation, playback, tempo, and display controls to practice.

You can edit the ABC and click Save to keep your changes.

You can use Browse to load another ABC file; the assigned instrument settings remain locked.

To return to normal use, open the Mountain Dulcimer Player without the lesson link.
