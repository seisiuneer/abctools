Instructions

Version 1.3

Overview

Uilleann Pipes Keyboard is a virtual musical instrument that lets you experience the sound of a full set of Irish Uilleann Pipes using a computer keyboard, mouse or touchscreen.

Based on my original iPad app, the tool features authentic samples recorded from actual instruments in four pitches: B, C, C♯ and D.

Play melodies on the piano-style keyboard, bend notes for expressive playing, accompany yourself with four regulator chords, and turn the drones on or off as you play.

Special F and C key selectors make it easier to play traditional Irish tunes using just the white piano keys, while the black keys provide access to the full chromatic range.

Adjust the individual instrument volumes, reverb and stereo drone chorus to create your own preferred sound.

The tool is designed for both casual musical exploration and expressive playing on computers and tablets.

Setting the Pitch of the Set

Use the Pitch selector to choose B, C, C♯ or D as the key of the set.

Playing the Chanter

Play the chanter on the piano keys with your mouse, fingers or computer keyboard.

Slide between piano keys to change notes without lifting your finger or releasing the mouse button.

Move your mouse or finger toward the bottom of a chanter piano key to bend the note down.

Use either vertical strip beside the upper controls to bend notes up or down.

Hold either Shift key while starting a chanter note on the computer keyboard to slide up into its pitch.

Hold Z or / on your computer keyboard to add finger vibrato, alternating between normal pitch and a slightly lower pitch.

Playing the Drones

Click or tap the central drone switch, or press Spacebar, to turn the drones on or off.

Playing the Regulators

Play the four regulator chords using the upper or lower parts of the two leftmost piano keys, or use either hand on the green computer keys in the home row below.

Hold a regulator key to sustain its chord while playing the chanter, or enable Hold in the Regulators group to sustain the last chord after release.

Press C to toggle regulator Hold and V to stop the regulators.

Chanter and Regulator Hold and Stop

Click or tap Hold in the Regulators group, or press C, to turn regulator Hold on or off.

Click or tap Hold in the Chanter group, or press the comma key, to turn chanter Hold on or off.

Chanter Hold is enabled by default and regulator Hold is disabled by default.

When Hold is on, the last note or chord in that section continues sounding after release until you play another in that section, turn its Hold off or use its Stop button.

With Hold enabled, play a sounding note or chord again to restart it from the beginning.

When Hold is off, notes or chords in that section stop when you release their keys.

Click or tap Stop in the Regulators group, or press V, to stop the regulators without changing regulator Hold.

Click or tap Stop in the Chanter group, or press M, to stop the chanter without changing chanter Hold.

Click or tap Stop All at the bottom center of the screen, or press Enter or Escape, to stop the chanter, regulators and drones without changing either Hold setting.

F and C Key Selectors

Use the selectors below the piano to choose whether the white F and C keys play natural or sharp notes.

Press B to toggle F / F♯ and N to toggle C / C♯.

For D major, select F♯ and C♯; for G major, select F♯ and C♮.

You can still play the black keys normally.

Setting the Volume and Effects Levels

Adjust the volume, reverb and drone chorus controls to taste.

Your settings are saved automatically.

Finger Vibrato and Pitch Bend Settings

Click or tap Settings at the lower right to adjust the finger vibrato rate (Hz), downward bend offset (cents), and maximum downward bend for each chanter note (cents).

The individual note bend settings follow the sounding note when using the F and C selectors.

Use the Shift Key Slide settings to choose the starting offset below the note (cents) and the time it takes to slide up into pitch (milliseconds), independently of the individual note bends.

The default Shift-key slide starts 20 cents below the note and takes 150 milliseconds.

Set the slide start offset from 0 to 100 cents and the bend time from 0 to 1000 milliseconds; setting either to zero disables Shift-key slides.

Use a vibrato rate from 0 to 10 Hz and bend values from 0 to 100 cents; a vibrato rate or offset of zero disables vibrato, and a key bend of zero disables that note’s downward key bend.

Click Defaults in this dialog to restore only the finger vibrato, individual note bend and Shift-key slide values.

This does not change the pitch, volumes, reverb, chorus, either Hold setting or F and C selectors on the main screen.

Click OK to apply and save your changes, or Cancel to leave the current settings unchanged.

Resetting All Settings to Defaults

Click or tap Defaults at the lower left of the screen, then choose Reset to restore all settings to their original values.

This resets the pitch of the set, volume and effects levels, both Hold settings, F and C selectors, finger vibrato rate and bend offset, all individual note bend settings, and Shift-key slide offset and time.

The restored settings are saved automatically.

Choose Cancel to leave your settings unchanged.

iOS and Safari

On iPhone, iPad and desktop Safari, click or tap Enable Playback before playing.
