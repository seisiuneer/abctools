SmeggTuner v1.4.5 — self-hosted under /tools/smeggtuner/

DEPLOYMENT
Upload the contents of the included `smeggtuner` directory to /tools/smeggtuner/
(or unzip the directory into your server's /tools/ directory).
Replace the prior SmeggTuner installation; keep the nested directories intact.

TEST NOTES
The direct /index.html route and dynamic assets must be verified with a
browser after deployment, including microphone permission and WebAssembly
engine analysis. Static asset checks cannot prove live microphone behavior.
If your server caches HTML/JS or serves precompressed .gz/.br assets,
replace/invalidate both old and new variants during upgrade.
